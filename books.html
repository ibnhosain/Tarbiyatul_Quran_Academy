The children's books

Reading · Book 1 65 pages · 23 sample pages

Easy Noorani Qaida — Step One

Your child's first Quran reading book. In 7 short lessons they learn all 29 letters, joining letters, the Makharij (where every sound comes from), Harakat, Madd, Jazm, Qalqalah, Tashdeed and Ghunnah — with big clear letters, practice boxes, quizzes and a progress chart.

  • 29 letters & letter families
  • 17 Makharij with clear tables
  • Harakat, Madd, Jazm, Tashdeed
  • Quizzes, review & certificate
Memorizing · Book 2 49 pages · 22 sample pages

My First Surahs — Memorized Surah

Seven Surahs (Al-Fatihah, Al-Kawthar, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Asr, An-Nasr, Al-Falaq, An-Nas) taught the way children really remember: every verse in little pieces, a Missing Word game, a simple meaning, a Little Talk with authentic sources, and a memorizing chart.

  • Verses split into little pieces
  • Missing Word memory game
  • Meaning of every verse
  • Revision plan & Surah chart
Duas & Adab · Book 3 69 pages · 25 sample pages

My Duas & Good Manners

18 lessons for everyday life — eating, salam, sleeping, the toilet, wudu, dressing, parents, the masjid, kindness and more. Every Dua is from the Quran or authentic Hadith with its source, every story is a true story, and every lesson ends with a My Good Deeds chart.

  • Authentic Duas with sources
  • True stories from Quran & Sunnah
  • 18 short Hadith + 5 pillars
  • Easy rules: Wudu, Ghusl, Tayammum
Writing · Book 4 · Print at home 50 pages · 24 sample pages

My First Arabic Writing Book

A printable A4 writing book built on handwriting research: big movements first (sky writing, finger, then pencil), only a little tracing, lots of writing on their own, and writing from memory. All 29 letters, dot games, and the first Arabic words — numbers, colours, body, fruits, food and days.

  • 5 writing steps for every letter
  • Warm-up lines, right to left
  • Dots, missing letters, matching
  • First Arabic words & certificate

How our teachers teach — the teacher guides

Parents often ask: “What actually happens in class?” These are the guides every teacher follows. (Originally written in Bangla for our teachers — shown here in English.)

For our teachers 10 pages · 11 sample pages

Teacher Guide — How We Teach Memorization

Every teacher follows this guide, so you know exactly what happens in class: eight proven memorization techniques (echo, little pieces, retrieval, backward chaining, spaced revision, bedtime reading, teach-back, meaning with gestures), a fixed class routine, gentle correction and strict care to teach only authentic Islam.

  • 8 memorization techniques
  • Step-by-step class routine
  • Never scold — gentle correction
  • Tips for each Surah
For our teachers 14 pages · 14 sample pages

Teacher Guide — Teaching Arabic Writing on Zoom

How our teachers teach handwriting online: what published research says (Longcamp 2005, James & Engelhardt 2012, Berninger 1997), the five steps for every letter, a second camera to watch your child's hand, the Zoom mirror problem solved, a 30-minute class plan, common mistakes and home games.

  • Research-based method
  • Second camera on the hand
  • 30-minute class plan
  • Common mistakes & solutions

Foundation & Beginner Texts

Absolute Beginner
Easy Qaida — Learning for Beginners
Academy-recommended edition

A gentle first Qaida for complete beginners — large, clear letters and simple steps for recognising the Arabic letters and their sounds with correct Makhraj from day one.

📚 Used in: Easy Noorani Qaida course
Beginner
Madani Qaida
Standard edition

A popular beginner Qaida with clear, large script — an excellent alternative foundational text for learning the Arabic letters, Makhraj, joining and basic reading.

📚 Used in: Noorani Qaida course
Core Text
Noorani Qaida
Molvi Noor Muhammad Ludhianvi

The most widely used foundational text for learning Arabic letters and beginning Quran reading. Covers the alphabet, vowels, joining rules and basic Quranic words with a clear, step-by-step structure trusted across generations.

📚 Used in: Noorani Qaida course
Supplementary
Juz Amma (30th Juz) — Colour-Coded Tajweed Edition
Various Publishers

A Tajweed colour-coded print of the 30th Juz — ideal for beginners learning to read short Surahs. The colour coding visually highlights which Tajweed rule applies to each word, making self-correction easier for students and parents.

📚 Used in: Quran Reading course
Mushaf
Holy Quran — Colour-Coded, 15-Line (with Tajweed Rules)
Various Publishers

The standard 15-line Mushaf with colour-coded Tajweed rules — used for full Quran reading. The colour coding shows which Tajweed rule applies to each word, helping students recite correctly and beautifully.

📚 Used in: Quran Reading & Quran with Tajweed courses

Tajweed Texts

Tajweed
Hidaayat ul Qari (هداية القاري)
Sheikh Abdul Fattah Al-Marsafi

A comprehensive and authoritative work on Tajweed rules. Covers all aspects of correct recitation — Makhraj, Sifat, Madd, Noon and Meem rules in systematic detail. Used as the primary reference for our advanced Tajweed course.

📚 Used in: Quran with Tajweed course
Tajweed
Al-Qawaid Al-Asasiyya fil Tajweed
Various

An accessible introduction to the core Tajweed rules — suitable for students transitioning from basic reading to structured Tajweed application. Concise rules with clear examples from the Quran.

📚 Used in: Quran with Tajweed course (early stages)

Islamic Studies & Deen Materials

Deen
Beheshti Zewar (Heavenly Ornaments)
Mawlana Ashraf Ali Thanwi

A foundational text for basic Islamic knowledge — covering Taharat, Salah, Sawm, Zakat and essential Masalah for everyday Muslim life. Sections are selected and adapted for age-appropriate teaching in the Deen & Tarbiyah taught in every course.

📚 Used in: every course (Deen & Tarbiyah)
Deen
Forty Hadith of Imam An-Nawawi
Imam An-Nawawi (with commentary)

Selected Ahadith from this classic collection form the basis of our character and manners teaching. Short, profound and highly applicable — ideal for children and teenagers to learn and memorise key prophetic teachings.

📚 Used in: every course (Deen & Tarbiyah)
Duas
Hisnul Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim)
Sa'eed ibn 'Ali ibn Wahf Al-Qahtani

The definitive collection of authentic Duas from the Quran and Sunnah for daily situations — the primary reference for teaching morning/evening Adhkar, situational Duas and protective supplications in our courses.

📚 Used in: every course (Deen & Tarbiyah) & supplementary reading
Academy Materials
Academy Moral-Education, Masail, Adab & Dua Books & Sheets
Tarbiyatul Quran Academy

A curated set of academy-prescribed books and printable sheets for moral education (Akhlaq), essential Masail, Adab and daily Duas — provided to students as part of every course's Deen & Tarbiyah.

📚 Used in: every course (Deen & Tarbiyah)
